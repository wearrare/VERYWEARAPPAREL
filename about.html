About

The Holy Haberdashery

Wear / Rare is a vintage curation practice built on one belief: what we wear is art, and the best of it is worth far more than its price tag.

Vintage is not a trend here. It is an archive. Every piece is sourced, studied, and chosen for what it is: the hand that made it, the decade it's from, and the way it carries itself.

He knows the industry deeply, especially high fashion and designer garments from houses like Chanel, Gucci, Christian Dior and Rick Owens. That knowledge shapes what comes into the collection, how it's presented, and how he styles the people who wear it.

Beyond selling, there's styling, closet edits, and Sip & Thrift evenings in the showroom, because clothes are better when people get to love them in the same room.
