Good to know

FAQ

How buying works, shipping, local pickup, international orders, and what to expect from vintage and used garments.

How It Works

How Do I Buy A Piece?
Tap a piece to see photos and measurements, then add it to your bag. At checkout you enter your name, email and address, and your shipping is added to the total. Then you pay with Venmo to @very_wear_apparel. Keep the shirt code in the Venmo note so we can match your payment.
Is A Piece Held When It Is In My Bag?
No. Every piece is one of one, and the first payment we receive gets it. If you pay for a piece that was already sold, we refund you in full.
Can I Make An Offer?
Yes. Tap a piece and choose Make An Offer. We reply within 24 hours. If we accept, we hold the piece for you for 24 hours so you can pay. Accepted offers are final. Shipping is added on top of the agreed price.
When Do I Hear From You?
We email you to confirm your order after your payment arrives. If you ship, your tracking number comes by email too.
How Do I Pay?
Venmo, to @very_wear_apparel. The amount is the price of your pieces plus shipping, shown on the payment screen. Your order is not final until we confirm your payment.

Shipping

How Much Is Shipping In The United States?
for 1 piece and for 2 to 3 pieces, paid at checkout. Orders ship by USPS Ground Advantage after your payment arrives. For 4 or more pieces, message us on Instagram for shipping.
Is Local Pickup Available?
Yes, and it is free for Columbia, SC and nearby towns. Choose Local Pickup at checkout and enter your town. After you pay, we email you to set a time and share the pickup address.
Can I Change My Address After I Order?
Yes. Use the link on the thank-you screen to send your shipping details again, or message us before your order ships.

International Shipping

Do You Ship Internationally?
Yes. Use the international order page. Choose your country and the shipping price appears in your total before you pay.
How Is International Shipping Priced?
By country, straight from the USPS price list for First-Class Package International Service. 1 piece uses the price for up to 1 lb. 2 to 3 pieces use the price for up to 3 lb. For 4 or more pieces, message us.
How Long Does It Take?
USPS does not publish a delivery time for this service. Delivery varies by destination and is not guaranteed. Tracking is available to select countries only.
Who Pays Customs, Duties And Taxes?
Your country may charge import duties, taxes and fees. These are not included in your order total. If they are not prepaid, the buyer usually pays them before the package is delivered. Other restrictions may apply based on the destination country, and you are responsible for knowing what your country allows.

Final Sale

What Is Your Return Policy?
All sales are final. There are no returns. If you have questions about a piece, please ask before you buy: message us on Instagram.
Do You Do Wholesale?
Yes. Message us on Instagram for wholesale.

About Vintage And Used Garments

Every Piece Is Pre-Owned Or Vintage
Every garment and accessory is pre-owned or vintage. Fading, small marks, stretching, pilling, soft spots, small repairs, and other signs of age are normal and part of the piece. We describe the condition and show photos of each piece, and we ask you to look closely at the photos before you buy.
Sold As Is
Pieces are sold as is, with no warranty of any kind, express or implied, including no warranty that a piece is fit for a particular purpose. Because every piece is used, it can have flaws we did not notice.
Sizing And Measurements
Vintage sizing is different from modern sizing, and tag sizes are often wrong or missing. Use the measurements listed on the piece, not the tag. Measurements are taken by hand with the garment laid flat, so they can vary slightly.
Colors And Photos
Colors can look different on different screens and in different light. Photos and descriptions on this site belong to Very Wear Apparel and may not be copied or reused without permission.
Care
Please wash or clean a piece before you wear it. Vintage fabric and prints can be delicate, so check any care label that is there, and consider washing gently or taking it to a cleaner. We are not responsible for damage that happens when a piece is washed or worn.
Materials And Sensitivities
Many vintage pieces have no fabric or care label. If you have allergies or fabric sensitivities, ask us about materials before you buy.
Brand Names And Authenticity
Brand names are used only to describe a piece. Very Wear Apparel is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any brand named on this site. We describe each piece honestly to the best of our knowledge, but we are not brand authenticators and we do not provide authentication certificates.

The Fine Print

Your Information
We use your name, email and address to fill your order and to contact you about it. Our Drop List emails are separate, and you can join or leave that list whenever you like.
Prices And Availability
Prices are in US dollars. Because every piece is one of one, a piece can sell before an order is finished. We can correct errors in a listing, a price, or a shipping amount, and will contact you first if your order is affected.
Questions
