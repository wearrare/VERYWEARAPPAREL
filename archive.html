The Archive

Some pieces are not for sale. They are held, studied, and shown, because a garment can be an artifact: a hand, a decade, a body that once moved through the world in it.

Clothing as collection

Price tells you what something costs. It doesn't tell you what it is. In the Archive, pieces are kept for their craft, their era, their cut, and the culture they carry, and shown the way a gallery shows a work.

Some archive pieces eventually find a new keeper. Most stay. Ask him about any plate.
